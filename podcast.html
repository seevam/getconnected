When AI Meets Compassion: Building Open Door for Migrant Workers

with Priya Menon, Founder of Open Door

In this episode, Priya shares how her daily commute past Singapore's Little India neighbourhood sparked an idea that became Open Door — an AI chatbot helping migrant workers navigate affordable resources, legal aid, and community support. She talks about the unexpected emotional weight of the problem, what she learned from listening before building, and why technology alone is never enough.
